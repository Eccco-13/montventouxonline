Privacy policy

In force from 18 November 2026. This English translation is provided for convenience; in case of difference, the French version prevails.

Data controller

Nicolas Lux EI (Mont Ventoux Online), 26 avenue François Raspail, 84810 Aubignan, France — contact@montventouxonline.fr.

The principle: collect as little as possible. Your rides stay on your computer; only the data needed for the account and the subscription ever leave your PC.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser: no advertising, no social media buttons, no fonts or resources loaded from another website, no Google Analytics. That is why no consent banner is shown to you.

Anonymous audience measurement

To know which pages are read and to improve the website, each page displayed sends our server (Cloudflare) an anonymous record: the page viewed, the language, the website you came from (its name only, for example google.com), the type of device (computer, tablet, phone) and the country deduced from the connection. Clicks on the download button and submissions of the contact form are counted in the same way.

Your IP address is not stored. To count the visitors of a day without identifying them, we only keep an encrypted, irreversible fingerprint, recalculated every day with a different key: it does not allow us to recognise you, to follow you from one day to the next, or to cross-reference this data with other data. These statistics are used only by the publisher, are passed on to no one and are deleted after 13 months. This measurement, strictly limited to the website's audience, is exempt from consent (recommendations of the CNIL, the French data protection authority).

This website

The website host, GitHub (GitHub Pages service, United States), keeps technical logs containing visitors' IP addresses for the security of its service. The publisher has no access to them and does not use them.

The contact form

When you write to us through the contact form, the following are recorded: the topic chosen, your name, your email address, the subject and text of your message and, if you provide them, technical details (game version, smart trainer, heart rate monitor, Windows version). To prevent abuse, a fingerprint of your IP address is also calculated (mixed with a secret value, it cannot be used to recover the address) and is used only to limit the number of messages sent within an hour.

Purpose: to reply to you and follow up on your request. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in answering the requests it receives, or pre-contractual steps taken at your request. Retention: 12 months after receipt, then automatic deletion. These messages are stored with Cloudflare (see below) and are read only by the publisher.

If you write directly to the contact email address, your message and your address are used only to reply to you, then kept for at most 3 years after the last exchange.

The game, on your computer

Your ride data (rider profile: first name, weight, height, FTP, maximum heart rate; power, cadence, heart rate, records, training log, GPX files, photos) are stored only on your computer. They are sent to no one. You delete them by uninstalling the game or deleting its data folder.

The GPX files of your rides are saved in your Documents folder: you decide whether or not to share them with other services.

The game queries the Open-Meteo weather service to show the day's weather on the route: only the route's coordinates are sent, no personal data.

The subscription, without an account

The subscription requires neither an account nor a password. When it is first opened, the game creates a random identifier for your computer. The publisher's subscription server records only: this identifier, the status of the subscription it is linked to, the subscription key (MVO-…) used to link a second computer, the game version and, to follow usage, the days on which the game was launched. It records neither your name nor your email address.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (providing the subscription) and, for usage, the publisher's legitimate interest. Retention: as long as the subscription exists, then 3 years after it ends; launch days are kept for 13 months.

Payment

Payment is handled by Stripe (Stripe Managed Payments, with Link), acting as reseller: Stripe processes your payment and billing data (name, email address, country, payment method) under its own privacy policy, and you manage or cancel your subscription in the Link customer area. The publisher never receives your bank details: its server only knows the identifier and the status of the subscription.

Service providers and transfers outside the European Union

The website is hosted by GitHub (United States); the subscription server, the contact form messages and the anonymous audience measurement are hosted by Cloudflare (United States); payment is entrusted to Stripe. These providers may process data outside the European Union. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR: an adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés), you may request access to your data, its rectification, erasure or portability, the restriction of its processing, or object to its processing, by writing to contact@montventouxonline.fr. You will receive an answer within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).
