Mont Ventoux via Bédoin

The royal road. The Tour de France route, the relentless forest and the desert of white stones. 21 kilometres from the vineyards to 1,912 metres.

Kilometre by kilometre

The climb profile

Each bar is one kilometre, coloured like the white and yellow roadside markers: green below 3%, yellow, orange, red, then dark red above 10%.

The climb

What lies ahead

Bédoin, 306 m

You leave the village square between plane trees and café terraces. The first six kilometres climb gently through vineyards and cherry orchards: time to find your rhythm.

Saint-Estève, the bend that changes everything

At the Saint-Estève bend the road enters the forest and the gradient stays above 9% for almost ten kilometres. In the game, your smart trainer makes you feel every metre.

Chalet Reynard, 1,417 m

The forest suddenly opens onto Chalet Reynard. The trees are gone: six kilometres remain through the scree, exposed to the wind, the observatory in sight.

The Tom Simpson memorial and the Col des Tempêtes

1.3 km from the top, the Tom Simpson memorial stands on the mountain side. Then the Col des Tempêtes, often swept by the Mistral, and the last 10% bend below the observatory, among the crowd.
